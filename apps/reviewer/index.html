Article Draft (14-Point Format)

TL;DR: Terraform execution plans are chronological diff lists, not structural topologies. To understand blast radius and systemic risk, platform teams must generate spatial topology graphs directly from AST and state.


Why this matters: When reviewing a 1,200-line plan file, humans mentally synthesize individual + and ~ line items. In large-scale AWS/GCP deployments, invisible dependency cascades lead to unexpected outages.


Implementation: Parse terraform plan -json to extract resource mutation nodes and reconstruct edge references directly into visual topology diagrams.
